Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find recipes based on my ingredients?

Enter your available ingredients in the recipe recommender page, separated by commas. The system will suggest recipes that match your ingredients.

How do I set my dietary preferences?

Go to the Settings page to save your dietary preferences. These will be automatically applied when searching for recipes.

Can I save my favorite recipes?

Yes! When you find a recipe you like, you can save it in your settings for quick access later.

How accurate are the calorie estimates?

Calorie estimates are based on standard nutritional databases. Actual calories may vary based on portion sizes and specific ingredients used.

Tips for Best Results

  • Be specific with your ingredients list
  • Set realistic calorie limits
  • Consider your cooking skill level
  • Use the dietary restrictions filter when needed
  • Save your preferences for faster searches
